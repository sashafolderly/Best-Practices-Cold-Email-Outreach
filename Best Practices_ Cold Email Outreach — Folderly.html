Setup & Infrastructure
1

Use a dedicated domain for outreach

Never send cold emails from your primary domain. A separate outreach domain protects your main domain's reputation from any negative signals.

2

Humanize your mailbox name

Avoid generic addresses like sales@ or info@. Use a real first name — e.g. dariia@yourdomain.com — to improve trust and open rates.

3

Polish your DNS records

Ensure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records are correctly configured on every sending domain before launching any outreach.

4

Ramp up sends gradually

Start with 10–20 cold emails per day and increase by 20 per day. Never exceed 200 cold emails per day per mailbox.

5

Add a delay between emails

Set a 180–200 second (3–3.5 minute) delay between individual sends to mimic human behavior and avoid spam filters.

6

Validate your lead database

Only send to verified, valid mailboxes. Any "catch-all" mailboxes should be uploaded to a separate sequence to avoid bounces damaging your sender score.

Email Content Rules
✅ Write in plain text — no HTML formatting in cold emails.
🚫 No spammy words in subject lines or body: $, free, trial, proposal, etc.
🚫 No links, images, videos, GIFs, emojis, PDFs, attachments, or bold text in the body, signature, or subject line of the initial email.
✅ Links, images, GIFs, and attachments can be included after the prospect replies — engagement signals make subsequent emails safer.
⚠️ Instead of an unsubscribe link, use a plain-text unsubscribe line (e.g. "Reply 'unsubscribe' to opt out"). This keeps you GDPR-compliant without the deliverability penalty that clickable unsubscribe links carry.
🚫 No broken symbols — characters like these:
  . These typically sneak in when copy-pasting from Google Docs, Word, or AI tools. Spam filters flag them as suspicious and they damage domain reputation. Stick to plain ASCII characters, especially in subject lines. Learn more →
